How it works

One plan. Two logins.
One shared shortlist.

From creating a profile to the first family meeting, here is how Soulvved works for the prospect and for the parents.

1

Create the profile

The prospect adds their story, education, career, values and preferences. Parents can add family background and horoscope details.

2

Invite the family

The prospect shares a code or link. Parents join with their own login under the same plan.

3

Discover and shortlist

The prospect swipes through suggestions. Parents browse detailed profiles. Everything goes into one shared shortlist with labels for who liked what.

4

Match

A match needs the prospect's like. Parents' likes act as strong suggestions, and both sides get alerts.

5

Families talk

After a mutual match, parent-to-parent chat unlocks. A meeting scheduler helps plan the first family meeting.

For the prospect

  • Swipe discovery with a smooth card stack: like, pass or shortlist.
  • Preference matching on age, career, location, values and deal-breakers.
  • See the profiles your parents liked, kept separate from your own picks.
  • Privacy controls: pause your profile or choose what is visible.

For parents

  • Detailed profiles with family background, education, career and horoscope.
  • Shortlist for your child. They see everything you do.
  • Match alerts the moment your child matches, and with whom.
  • Parent-to-parent chat and a meeting scheduler once there is a match.

One subscription

A single family plan covers the prospect's login and the parents' login. There is no second payment to bring the family in.
