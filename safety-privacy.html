Safety & privacy

Built for families,
careful with your data

Finding a partner is personal. Here is what we do, and what we will do, to keep the people and the information on Soulvved safe.

Contact details stay private

Phone numbers and other contact details stay hidden until both families agree to share them.

Chat only after a mutual match

Parent-to-parent chat unlocks only when the prospect has liked and been liked back. Nobody can message you out of the blue.

Report, block, moderate

You can report or block any profile or conversation at any time. Reports are reviewed by our team.

You control what is visible

Prospects can pause their profile or choose which details are shown. Only linked family members and your matches see your information, within the limits you set.

Data we collect

On this website, if you join the waitlist we ask for your name, email and whether you are a prospect or a parent. We may also use your approximate location, such as your district, from your network address, to understand where interest is coming from. We use this only to run early access and improve Soulvved. We do not sell your information.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information you gave us at any time by writing to us once our contact address is published here.

This page describes our approach during early access and will be updated with our full privacy policy before launch.
