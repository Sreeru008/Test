About us

Your choice.
Their blessing.

Soulvved is a matrimony platform built around a simple idea: in most Indian families, finding a life partner is not a solo decision, so the tool should not pretend it is.

Why we are building this

Most matrimony sites are built for one person. The prospect scrolls alone while parents forward profiles over WhatsApp, and everyone ends up with a different shortlist. Dating apps go the other way and leave families out completely. Neither matches how decisions actually happen at home.

Soulvved gives the prospect and the parents their own logins under one subscription. They share one shortlist, they can see who liked what, and the prospect always has the final say.

What the name means

Soul plus Wed. Two V shapes join to form a W, and the whole name reads as solved: finding your match, solved, together.

What we believe

  • Choice stays with the prospect. A match needs the prospect's like. Parents' likes are strong suggestions, never a decision.
  • Families are part of the process. Parent-to-parent chat opens only after a mutual match.
  • Privacy first. Contact details stay hidden until both sides agree.
  • Calm, honest design. No pressure tricks, no fake urgency.

Where we are

Soulvved is in early access. We are opening to a small group of families first so we can listen, fix what is wrong and build what matters. Early members get first access and early-member pricing.
